3. What PAL Is — and What It Is Not

PAL is a health-information and self-management companion. It helps you ask health questions in your own language, view the Care Plan your doctor has written, keep a personal health record, receive medication reminders, and request appointments with your clinic.

Important — Please read

PAL is not a medical device and does not provide a medical diagnosis, treatment, or professional medical advice. AI Output is information and decision support only. It does not replace consultation with a qualified healthcare professional, and it does not create a doctor–patient relationship between you and LocumPro.

Always seek the advice of your doctor or a qualified professional for any medical concern, and never disregard or delay professional advice because of something you read in PAL.

3.1 Emergencies

PAL is not for emergencies. If you may be experiencing a medical emergency, or thoughts of harming yourself, contact your local emergency services or a crisis helpline immediately. PAL includes automated safety prompts that may display emergency contact information, but these do not constitute emergency care and must not be relied upon in place of it.

3.2 Clinical content is your doctor’s

Where PAL shows your Care Plan, prescriptions or nutrition plan, that content is authored by your treating doctor or clinic and is displayed to you as written. PAL does not alter, rewrite, or override clinical instructions. If Care Plan content appears inaccurate or out of date, contact your clinic — PAL cannot change it.

3.3 Imaging

PAL does not interpret medical images such as X-rays, scans or MRIs. Such images require review by a qualified radiologist or your doctor.
