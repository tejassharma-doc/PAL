Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12th October, 2026 · LocumPro Health Private Limited

This Privacy Policy explains how LocumPro Health Private Limited (“LocumPro”, “we”, “us” or “our”) collects, uses, shares, protects, and otherwise processes personal data in connection with PAL — Personal Assistant for Life (the “App” or the “Services”), including its mobile application and related services.

It should be read together with our Terms and Conditions (the “Terms”) and forms part of them. Capitalised terms not defined here have the meaning given in the Terms. It is published in compliance with, among other laws, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”) and the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the rules framed thereunder, in each case as amended.

PLEASE READ THIS PRIVACY POLICY CAREFULLY. By registering for, subscribing to, accessing, or using PAL, or by otherwise providing personal data to us, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the Services.

1. Introduction and Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to all Users of PAL — individuals who download and use the App, Family Caregivers who manage profiles for others, and, where PAL is deployed by a Healthcare Organisation, the patients of that organisation. It covers personal data processed through the mobile application and any related interfaces or communications.

PAL is a patient-facing health-companion application. It may connect to your clinic’s systems — including Vaide, LocumPro’s clinic platform — to surface your appointments and the Care Plan your doctor has written. This shapes how privacy responsibilities are shared, as explained in Clause 2.

2. Our Roles: Data Fiduciary and Data Processor

The key distinction

For most consumers who download PAL directly and subscribe, LocumPro is the Data Fiduciary for your PAL account and the data you put into the App. Where a Healthcare Organisation deploys PAL for its patients, that organisation is the Data Fiduciary and LocumPro acts as a Data Processor on its instructions. Clinical content that originates from your clinic remains under your clinic’s responsibility.

2.1 Consumer model — LocumPro as Data Fiduciary

When you download PAL and subscribe as an individual (including under a Family Plan you control), LocumPro is the Data Fiduciary for your account data, subscription and payment data, the conversations you have with PAL, and the health information you choose to add or upload. For this data, you exercise your rights directly with us under Clause 14, and we rely on your consent and the other lawful bases described in this Policy.

2.2 Organisation-deployed model — LocumPro as Data Processor

Where a hospital, clinic, or healthcare network deploys PAL for its patients (with its own administration and, in some cases, its own AI configuration), that organisation determines the purposes of processing and is the Data Fiduciary. LocumPro processes patient data only on the organisation’s documented instructions and in accordance with the Terms and any data-processing agreement. In that case, the organisation’s own privacy notice also applies, and certain rights are exercised through the organisation.

2.3 Clinic-sourced clinical content

Your Care Plan, prescriptions, and appointment information displayed in PAL originate from your treating clinic’s systems (including Vaide). Your clinic remains responsible, as the fiduciary for its own records, for the accuracy of that clinical content. PAL displays it to you — with your involvement and, where personal, your consent — and does not alter it.

3. Key Definitions

• “Personal Data” means any data about an individual who is identifiable by or in relation to such data.

• “Health Data” means personal data relating to your health, including symptoms, conditions, medications, lab results, Care Plan content, and the health questions you ask.

• “Data Principal” means the individual to whom Personal Data relates (that is normally you, or a Family Member you manage).

• “Data Fiduciary” and “Data Processor” have the meanings given under the DPDP Act, as described in Clause 2.

• “Sub-Processor” means a third party we engage to help provide the Services, as described in Clause 8.

• “Family Caregiver” means a User who manages a profile for another person under a Family Plan, with authority to do so.

5. How We Use Your Data

We use personal data to:

• provide, personalise, and maintain the Services, including answering your questions and showing your Care Plan, appointments, and reminders;

• read your personal record to answer a personal question — only after you consent for that conversation (see Clause 6);

• set up and process your Subscription, including recurring billing, renewals, and refunds;

• operate automated safety prompts that may display emergency contact information when your message suggests a possible emergency or risk of harm;

• communicate with you about the Services, including service, security, and billing messages;

• maintain security, prevent fraud and abuse, and keep audit logs (see Clause 11);

• improve the Services using aggregated or de-identified data that does not identify you; and

• comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms.

6. Consent and Your Control

By default, PAL answers your questions using publicly available health information and does not read your personal health record. When a question is about you, PAL shows a consent request before accessing your record.

• Per-conversation consent. Permission applies to that conversation only, unless you choose a standing “always allow” setting in Settings.

• “Keep it general.” You can decline record access and still receive a general answer.

• Withdrawal. You can withdraw a standing permission at any time. Withdrawal is prospective and does not affect processing already carried out lawfully.

• Actions need confirmation. PAL takes no action (such as booking) without your explicit confirmation.

7. How the AI Processes Your Data

To answer your questions, PAL may consult several specialised AI components (for example, ones focused on your record, medications, nutrition, evidence, and appointments) and combine their outputs into a single answer using an AI model provided by a sub-processor. Only the components relevant to your question are used, and your personal record is used only with your consent.

7.1 Reuse of general answers (semantic cache)

Important — how answer reuse works

To answer common questions quickly and consistently, PAL may store and reuse previously generated general health answers, and may match questions that mean the same thing even across different languages. This reuse applies to general health information only.

Answers that draw on your personal record are not placed in this shared cache and are not reused for other Users. Your record is never shared with another User.

7.2 Emergency and safety detection

PAL applies automated checks to detect messages that may indicate an emergency or risk of self-harm, in order to display emergency contact information promptly. This is a safety feature, not medical care, and does not replace emergency services.

7.3 Voice

Where you use voice input, your audio is transcribed to text so PAL can answer. Depending on your device, transcription may occur on your device; where it is processed by a service provider, that provider acts as a sub-processor under Clause 8.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it to serve third-party advertising.

9. Cross-Border Data Transfers

Some sub-processors (for example, certain AI or infrastructure providers) may process data outside India. Where this happens, we take steps required by applicable law to protect your data, and such transfers are subject to any restrictions or notified-country requirements under the DPDP Act. Healthcare Organisations that deploy PAL may choose configurations that keep processing within a specific environment.

10. Family Access and Children’s Data

A Family Caregiver may manage a profile for another person under a Family Plan. The Caregiver must have authority to act for that person and must use their data only for their benefit. For a Family Member who is a child (a person under 18), the parent or lawful guardian is responsible for providing verifiable consent as required under the DPDP Act 2023 and for supervising use. We do not knowingly process a child’s data for tracking, behavioural monitoring, or targeted advertising.

Access granted to a Caregiver is scoped and revocable, and each person’s record is kept separate. Where access is revoked, it stops prospectively.

11. Audit Logs

Every access to a health record within PAL — by you, by PAL with your consent, or by an authorised Family Caregiver — generates an audit log entry designed to be tamper-evident and not alterable through the App. You may request a copy of the log entries relating to your record. Logs support security, accountability, and your rights, and are retained as described in Clause 13.

12. What We Never Do

• We never sell your personal data.

• We never use your data to serve third-party advertising.

• We never share one User’s health record with another User.

• We never read your personal record to answer a general question, or a personal question without your consent for that conversation.

Operator/support staff manage the system; the App is designed so that they do not have access to your personal health record content in the ordinary course.

13. Data Retention

We retain personal data for as long as needed to provide the Services to you, and thereafter only as required to meet legal, regulatory, tax, accounting, security, or dispute-resolution obligations, after which it is deleted or de-identified. You can delete individual conversations at any time, and you can delete your account, subject to any retention we are legally required to apply.

14. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, and where LocumPro is the Data Fiduciary (Clause 2.1), you may:

• access a summary of the personal data we process about you;

• seek correction or updating of inaccurate or incomplete data;

• seek erasure of your data where it is no longer required and retention is not legally mandated;

• withdraw a consent you previously gave (prospectively);

• nominate another individual to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity, as provided under the DPDP Act; and

• raise a grievance with our Grievance Officer, and escalate to the Data Protection Board of India where permitted.

Where PAL is deployed by a Healthcare Organisation (Clause 2.2), please exercise these rights through that organisation; we will assist it as its Data Processor. To make a request to us, contact us at inir@medmode.org.

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal data, including access controls, encryption in transit, tenant/record isolation between users and organisations, and audit logging. No system is perfectly secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect your data and to respond appropriately to any incident, including notifying you and the authorities where required by law.

16. Cookies and Analytics

The App uses only the storage and diagnostic mechanisms necessary to operate the Services reliably and securely (for example, keeping you signed in and understanding crashes and performance). We do not use them for third-party advertising.
