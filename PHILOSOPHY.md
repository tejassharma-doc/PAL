# PAL — Philosophy

PAL is a patient-owned health record. The patient is the primary beneficiary — not the institution, not the AI provider.

## Core Principles

1. **Patients own their data.** Records belong to the patient. Institutions operate the platform; they do not own the data.
2. **Privacy is the default, not the exception.** PHI stays on host unless the patient explicitly consents to egress. Consent is per-session by default; standing consent is opt-in and revocable.
3. **AI proposes; humans decide.** No clinical decision, booking, or message is taken without human confirmation. The AI is a thinking partner, not an actor.
4. **Honest uncertainty over false confidence.** "No good evidence found" is a valid and correct answer. Confabulation is never acceptable.
5. **Transparency is non-negotiable.** "Why do you think that?" must always be answerable. Every fact carries its evidence class and provenance chain.
6. **Safety is a hard constraint, not a heuristic.** Emergency/crisis detection short-circuits before clinical reasoning. Imaging is never interpreted. Prescriptions are never made.
